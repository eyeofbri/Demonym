# Public Release History

This is the shorter public version history. My private repo has the smaller alpha and patch builds in between these releases.

## v0.9.22.2 - Splash Screen Update

This is a small presentation/documentation update on top of the v0.9.22 content-lock build. It adds the newer animated Demonym title/splash screen and the on-device screenshot shortcut used for the current public screenshots. Hold **Fn/Opt + P** to save a 240x135 PNG to `/demonym/screenshots/` on the SD card.

The main gameplay systems are otherwise still on the v0.9.22 content-lock baseline.

## v0.9.22 - Balance + Content Lock

This is the current late-alpha snapshot. I stopped adding major mechanics here and moved into balance, cleanup, content completion, and testing. The main areas are economy, Energy, Hunger, Stress, healing, Salvage, Roam rewards, battle XP, Retreat, Depth scaling, Legacy inheritance, room content, creature behavior, encounters, and Signalpedia entries.

## v0.9 - Carry + Environment

Added the first larger pass at motion/environment systems, territory/environment experiments, hardware settings, and the groundwork that eventually led into the later multiplayer and portability work.

## v0.8 - World + Progression

Roam got much larger and more structured. This series also added Adaptations, Pressures, more Training programs, Signalpedia work, longer-term creature history, and more visible progression.

## v0.7 - Economy + Lineages

Added the Supply Exchange, Crossings, more Training content, Resonance progression, and the expanded eight-Lineage creature system.

## v0.6 - Battle + Roam

Added the first full Battle and Roam loops, then built them out with moves, items, more battle presentation, Rest activities, settings, and early MVP cleanup.

## v0.5 - Adult Evolution

Juveniles could finally evolve into persistent Adult forms. This series also added departure/replacement behavior and more complete Habitat presentation.

## v0.4 - Training + Evolution Setup

Added the reusable Training structure through Signal Drift, records, development scoring, and the first complete path toward Adult evolution.

## v0.3 - Care + Temperament

Turned the early prototype into more of a virtual creature game with needs, hidden temperament, care actions, menu routing, Inventory, Coins, and the first larger Habitat UI.

## v0.2 - Persistent Life

Added persistent creature identity, authenticated saves, Egg and Juvenile stages, hatching, Habitat/Status screens, and the first version of creature cries.

## v0.1 - One Demonym

The starting point. This was mainly the deterministic procedural creature generator and the first experiments with small generated Wisp sprites.
