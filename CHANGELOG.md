# Public Release History

This is a **curated milestone history** for the public Demonym repository. The private development repository contains a much finer-grained sequence of alpha and patch snapshots.

Public firmware releases focus on versions that represent a meaningful stage of the project rather than every internal patch.

## v0.9.22 — Balance + Content Lock

Demonym reaches its late-alpha content-lock milestone. Major new mechanics are frozen while the existing game is balanced, completed, and stabilized.

Highlights include final-pass tuning for needs, Energy, sleep, healing, economy, Salvage, Roam rewards, battle XP, Retreat, Depth scaling, and Signal Legacy inheritance; expanded room/biome identity; Habitat-object and creature behavior copy; and additional Signalpedia field behavior and field notes.

## v0.9 — Carry + Environment

Established the v0.9 generation of the project: mature creature persistence, environmental/carry behavior, expanded Training and Roam systems, Signalpedia, Local Link multiplayer, progressively hardened storage, and the foundation that later became Signal Legacy and content lock.

## v0.8 — World & Progression

Expanded Roam into a larger scrolling procedural space and layered in deeper progression systems including Adaptations, Pressures, status effects, additional Training programs, Signalpedia foundations, richer visual history, and environmental behavior.

## v0.7 — Economy & Lineages

Added the Supply Exchange economy, surprise Crossings, additional Training programs, Resonance progression, and the eight distinct procedural Lineages that define Demonym's creature families.

## v0.6 — Battle & Roam

Opened the first major post-evolution gameplay loop with deterministic turn-based Battle and procedural Roam expeditions, including encounters, bosses, merchants, rewards, injury, and recovery.

## v0.5 — Adult Evolution

Completed the first full creature lifecycle milestone. Juveniles could evolve into persistent Adult forms while retaining identity and history across the transition.

## v0.4 — Training & Evolution

Introduced the reusable Training architecture with Signal Drift, persistent performance records, and development scoring used to bridge the Juvenile and Adult stages.

## v0.3 — Care & Temperament

Turned the procedural organism into a virtual creature with needs, temperament, active-time progression, care actions, a growing menu system, inventory, economy groundwork, and richer egg interaction.

## v0.2 — Persistent Life

Added persistent identity and the first real lifecycle: Egg and Juvenile stages, Habitat and Status views, authenticated save data, observation, hatching, and the first creature continuity across restarts.

## v0.1 — One Demonym

The original procedural visual prototype: deterministic 32×32 creature generation, seeded morphology, palette and outline logic, form variation, simple animation, and host-side generator validation.
